Tapsilog

Description

Tapsilog is a popular Filipino breakfast meal made with beef tapa, garlic fried rice, and a fried egg. It is commonly served with vinegar as a dipping sauce.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings
15 minutes 20 minutes 2 people

Ingredients

  • 250 grams of beef
  • 2 cups of cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tablespoons of soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon of vinegar
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper
