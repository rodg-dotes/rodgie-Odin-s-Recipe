Champorado

Description

Champorado is a traditional Filipino chocolate rice porridge made with glutinous rice and cocoa. It is usually served warm with milk and is commonly enjoyed for breakfast or as a snack.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings
15 minutes 20 minutes 2 people

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of glutinous rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of evaporated milk
  • A pinch of salt
